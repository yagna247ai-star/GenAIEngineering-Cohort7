# FAQs

**What is the Lead-to-Meeting AI Agent?**
An AI that greets visitors, answers product questions from your KB, qualifies with BANT, and books meetings for Hot leads instantly.

**How is this Kore.ai?**
Built on Kore.ai XO principles: Storyboard dialogs, Search AI RAG (grounded KB), Agent AI tools (score, CRM, book, notify).

**What data do you need?**
Product PDFs, pricing sheet, 3 case studies, BANT weights, CRM token.

**Privacy?**
PII masked in logs, GDPR opt-in for nurture, no training on your data without consent.
